 About the Project

SOPDET FARMSHIELD is an interactive crop rotation exploration tool designed to help farmers explore and compare crop rotation plans across four growing seasons.

The platform brings together historical climate data, crop reference profiles, and rule-based checks to help farmers understand how crop selection, growing seasons, and botanical families relate to one another.

Inspired by Sopdet, the ancient Egyptian name associated with the star Sirius, the project connects Egypt's agricultural heritage with modern Earth observation data.

 The Problem

Planning crop rotations involves considering several connected factors, including seasonal conditions, crop characteristics, botanical families, and climate conditions.

When this information is presented separately, comparing different crop rotation plans can become difficult.

SOPDET FARMSHIELD brings relevant information together in one interactive platform, helping farmers explore alternative crop rotation scenarios and identify potential issues.

 Key Features

- Farm Setup: Select and explore agricultural locations.
- Climate Dashboard: Explore historical climate information through interactive charts and visualizations.
- Crop Rotation Planner: Explore crop rotation plans across four growing seasons.
- Scenario Comparison: Compare current farming practices with two alternative scenarios.
- Botanical Family Checks: Highlight repeated crop families across growing seasons.
- Seasonal Alerts: Identify potential mismatches between selected crops and their growing seasons.
- Interactive Dashboard: Explore key indicators and insights in one place.

 How It Works

1. Select a Location: Choose an available agricultural location.
2. Explore Climate Data: Review historical climate information for the selected location.
3. Create Rotation Scenarios: Explore crop selections across four growing seasons.
4. Review Alerts: Examine potential seasonal mismatches and repeated botanical families.
5. Compare Scenarios: Compare different plans to better understand their characteristics.

 Data Sources and Methodology

- NASA POWER: Historical climate data used to explore environmental conditions.
- FAO-Sourced Crop References: Crop information organized into reference profiles for the selected locations.
- Rule-Based Logic: Transparent checks designed to highlight repeated botanical families and potential seasonal mismatches.

Climate information and crop reference values are handled as separate data categories when their definitions or measurement contexts are not directly comparable. This helps avoid unsupported calculations.

 Development Challenges and Solutions

1. Integrating Different Data Sources

Climate information and crop reference profiles have different structures and purposes.

Our Approach: We organized the information into structured data formats and connected the relevant data to the platform's dashboard and planning features.

2. Making Crop Rotation Checks Understandable

Crop rotation involves more than selecting crops. Repeated botanical families and unsuitable growing seasons can create potential planning issues.

Our Approach: We implemented transparent, rule-based checks that highlight these conditions through alerts, making the results easier to understand.

3. Comparing Multiple Farming Scenarios**

Comparing different plans across four seasons can be difficult when information is displayed separately.

Our Approach: We developed a scenario comparison interface supported by dashboards and visualizations, allowing users to examine the plans in an organized way.

 Future Development

SOPDET FARMSHIELD is a starting point for further development. Planned improvements include:

- Expanding the platform to support more agricultural locations and crop profiles.
- Improving crop reference documentation and data validation.
- Enhancing crop rotation evaluation logic with additional agricultural factors.
- Adding more detailed climate analysis and comparison tools.
- Allowing users to save and share crop rotation scenarios.
- Improving usability, accessibility, and application testing.

 Project Scope and Limitations

SOPDET FARMSHIELD is an exploratory tool designed to support farmers in understanding and comparing crop rotation scenarios. It does not replace the farmer's experience or decision-making.

The platform uses historical climate information rather than real-time data or weather forecasts. Its rule-based checks highlight potential issues but do not guarantee the success or failure of a crop rotation plan.
